teaching

Courses, supervision and teaching roles at the University of Southern Denmark, Swansea University and ETH Zürich.

Most of my teaching is in subjects that students learn by applying them: programming, statistics for data science, computational physics and high-performance computing. In these courses, I combine short lectures with exercise sessions in which the students write and run their own code. In more theoretical courses, such as partial differential equations and complex analysis, I use blackboard lectures and problem classes. I use the high-performance-computing systems from my research in teaching, so that students gain practical experience with parallel computing and large data sets.

In 2025, I was nominated for the Faculty of Science teaching award.

Supervision

Where possible, I connect Bachelor’s and Master’s projects to ongoing research, so that students work with real data and with the computing systems used in research. I find that a combination of independent work and short, regular meetings gives the best outcome.
