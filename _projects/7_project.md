*DIAS and SDU, 2017–today*

As Fellow (2017–2023) and Senior Fellow (since 2024) of the Danish Institute for Advanced Study, I take part in projects with the faculties of Health Sciences, Humanities, and Business and Social Sciences. In these projects, I contribute methods from computational science and statistics.

- **2024–2025:** Can large language models reliably extract medical data from large patient datasets? With the Chiropractors' Knowledge Centre Odense.
- **Since 2024:** Member of the [AI Learning Lab](https://www.sdu.dk/da/forskning/stem/fnug-labs/ai-learning-lab/medarbejdere).
- **Since 2023:** Computational expert in [DRIVEN](https://www.sdu.dk/en/forskning/driven/medlemmer), the Center for Behaviour Change at the Faculty of Health Sciences.
- **Since 2021:** Member of the [Center for Learning Computational Thinking](https://www.sdu.dk/en/forskning/lct/people).
- **2020–2023:** DIAS seed funding, *Toward a cognitive history of pre-modern rhetoric as mathematical thinking*, with the Faculty of Humanities.
- **2020–2023:** DIAS seed funding, *Measuring the impact of interdisciplinary research*, with the Faculty of Business and Social Sciences.

**Quantum Kate.** At SDU, I contributed to *Quantum Kate* (*Kvante Karina* in Danish), a YouTube series that explains topics in physics for teenagers, from the Standard Model of particle physics to dark matter {% cite Jager:2018crv %}. One aim of the project is to increase the representation of women in physics by providing relatable role models. The series received an award for the best outreach project at an international physics conference.
