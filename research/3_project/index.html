Hadrons in thermal QCD

FASTSUM, parity doubling and the thermal transition

FASTSUM, 2013–today

Within the FASTSUM collaboration, we study hadrons across the transition to the quark–gluon plasma using anisotropic lattices with Nf = 2+1 flavours of Wilson quarks. The small temporal lattice spacing provides more Euclidean-time points at a given temperature than an isotropic lattice.

We have shown that the positive- and negative-parity partners of the nucleon and of strange baryons, which have different masses at low temperature, become approximately degenerate above the transition (Aarts et al., 2015; Aarts et al., 2017; Aarts et al., 2019). This parity doubling is a direct signal of chiral symmetry restoration in the baryonic sector. In a related project with the Mainz group, we studied charge transport and vector-meson dissociation in two-flavour QCD (Brandt et al., 2016).

With the Gen2L ensembles (mπ ≈ 239 MeV), we determined properties of the thermal transition (Aarts et al., 2022), open-charm mesons and charmed baryons (Aarts et al., 2022; Aarts et al., 2024), the curvature of the pseudocritical line (Smecca et al., 2025) and the effective restoration of U(1)A symmetry (Aarts et al., 2026).

Contribution. Generation of gauge configurations and correlation functions; baryon correlators on the Gen2 ensembles and their analysis; since 2023, the computing applications as PI of several EuroHPC and DeiC allocations.

Outlook. The main limitation of these results is that the pion mass is above its physical value and that only one temporal lattice spacing is available at the lighter pion mass. Therefore, we cannot yet separate the light-quark-mass dependence from temporal discretisation effects. The next generation of ensembles (Gen2P, Gen3L and Gen3P) is designed to remove these two limitations. Note that the spatial lattice spacing is approximately the same for all ensembles, so that this programme does not yet allow a continuum extrapolation.
