Nucleon structure

Excited states, axial charge and form factors

CLS / Mainz, 2010–2019

The nucleon axial charge gA is known precisely from neutron β-decay and is therefore a benchmark for lattice calculations of nucleon structure. At the time of our calculation, many lattice determinations were systematically below the experimental value. We showed that contributions from excited states are a significant source of systematic error at the source–sink separations used at the time, and that the summation method allows them to be controlled (Capitani et al., 2012).

We subsequently applied the same strategy to the electromagnetic and axial form factors of the nucleon (Capitani et al., 2015; Capitani et al., 2019). In addition, I worked on improved interpolating operators for hadrons at non-zero momentum and on the shape of smeared sources (Della Morte et al., 2012; von Hippel et al., 2013). The explicit treatment of excited states is now a standard part of nucleon-structure calculations.

Contribution. Computation of nucleon two- and three-point functions, smeared sources and interpolating fields at non-zero momentum, and the excited-state analysis.
