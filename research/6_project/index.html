One-flavour QCD and super Yang-Mills

Large N_c, orientifold equivalence and the SYM spectrum

Odense lattice group, 2021–today

QCD with quarks in the two-index antisymmetric representation (the Corrigan–Ramond extension) coincides with ordinary QCD for Nc = 3. At large Nc, the theory with one Dirac flavour is related by orientifold planar equivalence to the bosonic sector of 𝒩 = 1 super Yang–Mills theory.

We computed the spectrum of one-flavour QCD and compared it with the expectations from this correspondence (Della Morte et al., 2023; Ziegler et al., 2022; Jäger et al., 2023). Since the size of the 1/Nc corrections at Nc = 3 is not known a priori, we have started simulations at larger Nc (Martins et al., 2024) and studied the definition of the topological charge in these theories (Butti et al., 2025).

Contribution. Simulation set-up and production, and supervision of the SDU-based junior researchers in the project.
