*Odense lattice group, 2021–today*

QCD with quarks in the two-index antisymmetric representation (the Corrigan–Ramond extension) coincides with ordinary QCD for N<sub>c</sub> = 3. At large N<sub>c</sub>, the theory with one Dirac flavour is related by orientifold planar equivalence to the bosonic sector of 𝒩 = 1 super Yang–Mills theory.

We computed the spectrum of one-flavour QCD and compared it with the expectations from this correspondence {% cite DellaMorte:2023ylq Ziegler:2021nbl Jaeger:2022ypq %}. Since the size of the 1/N<sub>c</sub> corrections at N<sub>c</sub> = 3 is not known a priori, we have started simulations at larger N<sub>c</sub> {% cite Martins:2023kcj %} and studied the definition of the topological charge in these theories {% cite Butti:2025rnu %}.

**Contribution.** Simulation set-up and production, and supervision of the SDU-based junior researchers in the project.
