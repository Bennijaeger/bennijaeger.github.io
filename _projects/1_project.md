*CLS / Mainz, 2010–2017*

The hadronic vacuum polarisation gives the leading hadronic contribution to the anomalous magnetic moment of the muon, a<sub>μ</sub> = (g−2)<sub>μ</sub>/2, and dominates the uncertainty of its Standard Model prediction. In my PhD project, we computed this contribution with two flavours of O(a)-improved Wilson quarks on CLS ensembles {% cite DellaMorte:2011aa %}. The main difficulty is the region of small momentum transfer, which dominates the integral but is poorly resolved on a finite lattice. We used twisted boundary conditions to obtain additional momenta in this region and studied chiral, cutoff and finite-size effects.

Subsequently, we derived a representation of the Adler function in terms of the spatially summed vector correlator {% cite Francis:2013fzp %}. Time-momentum representations of this kind avoid the fit of the vacuum polarisation function at small momenta and are now used in most lattice determinations of the hadronic contribution, including our 2017 result {% cite DellaMorte:2017dyu %}.

**Contribution.** Main topic of my PhD thesis {% cite Jager:2014iaq %}: computation and analysis of the vector correlators, momentum resolution with twisted boundary conditions, and the time-momentum representation of the Adler function.
